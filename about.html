About Apt Controls

Three decades of instrumentation expertise, built on ethics, superior quality and total customer satisfaction.

Our Story

From a Partnership Firm in 1993 to a Trusted Instrumentation House

Apt Controls was established as a partnership firm in 1993 in Dewas, on the A.B. Road corridor of Madhya Pradesh's busiest industrial belt. From day one, we envisioned a success route based on ethics and superior quality — a principle that still guides every quotation, delivery and service call.

Today we are an independent, family-owned company engaged in the distribution of industrial process control instruments, electrical, electronics and automation products, representing world-wide leading brands in the industrial market. Manufacturing plants across the Dewas–Indore region and central India rely on us for genuine products, honest advice and support that continues long after the sale.
